In May 2009, developer Markus Persson (Notch) made a simple test game called "Cave Game." The first version was just a flat world made of grass and cobblestone blocks. Notch added basic building features, changed the name to Minecraft, and released the very first playable version to the public on May 17, 2009.

Indev was initially released on December 23, 2009 after Notch received requests to let the community try out new features. Indev is also the oldest version of Minecraft. Indev version 0.31 was put to the public at minecraft.net/indev and available only to people who had purchased the game.

Infdev released on February 27, 2010 and is only playable in single-player Survival mode. Infdev succeeded Indev when Notch decided to rewrite the game's code to allow terrain to be "infinite" rather than finite. The edge of the map limits the world to 64,000,000 blocks by 64,000,000 blocks, or 4.096 billion square kilometers.

The Alpha version of Minecraft was released in June 2010. In Minecraft Alpha, there was only Survival mode playable in this version. During the Alpha phase, Minecraft was updated very frequently due to Notch working full-time on Minecraft since June 1, 2010, Minecraft Alpha also introduced The Nether - A realm that is very distinct from the Overworld (basically hell) with extraordinary mobs and structures such as Zombie Pigmen, Wither Skeleton and Nether Fortress.

December 2010

The Minecraft Beta (December 2010–2011) bridged the game's early sandbox days and its official release, introducing the foundations of modern gameplay. It added core mechanics like hunger, sprinting, and experience points, alongside Creative Mode and dynamic weather. This period also brought the world to life with iconic mobs like Endermen and Wolves, plus essential structures like NPC villages, mineshafts, and strongholds.

Minecraft 1.0.0 is the first full version of the game, was released on November 18, 2011 during MINECON 2011, marking the game as officially released after two and a half years of development. This version adds brewing, enchanting, the End, hardcore mode, breeding, and more. It is the last version of Minecraft Markus Persson (Notch) developed before he gave creative development to Jens Bergensten.

October 2013

Minecraft Version 1.7.2 "The Update that Changed the World" is a major update to Minecraft (Java Edition) released on October 25, 2013, which added many new biomes, new generated structures, new flowers, stained glass, better networking, new commands and more.

The Combat Update is the name for Java Edition version 1.9, a major update released on Monday, February 29, 2016. This update overhauled the combat system, among other additions and changes: a method of restarting the ender dragon boss fight, Shield, spectral arrow, 14 types of tipped arrow and a lingering potion for each type of potion, Elytra for Gliding.

April 2019

1.14, the release of Village & Pillage and the Texture Update, is a major update to Java Edition released on April 23, 2019. It focuses mainly on villages, adding a new type of illagers known as pillagers, and redesigns village architecture to match the biome it is located in. This update also introduces many new blocks and mobs, revamps the crafting system by moving some functionalities to different blocks, updates the taiga biome with foxes and sweet berries, and adds a new bamboo jungle biome along with bamboo and pandas. It is also the first major release to include the new textures of the Texture Update.

Caves & Cliffs is the name of two major themed updates for Java and Bedrock editions. The first part was released as Java Edition 1.17 and Bedrock Edition 1.17.0 on June 8, 2021, which focused on blocks and mobs. The second part was released as Java Edition 1.18 and Bedrock Edition 1.18.0 on November 30, 2021, and brought revamped overworld generation, including new caves and mountains.
