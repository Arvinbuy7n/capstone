The Nether

The Nether is a hazardous, hell-like alernate dimension accessed by building and lighting an obsidian portal. Characterized by vast lava oceans, and red fog, this wasteland is filled with unique resources like Netherite and Quartz, but it is also home to dangerous mobs like Ghasts and Blazes. The standard rules of The Nether are: water evaporates easily, and trying to sleep in a bed causes a catastrophic explosion. Additionally, Nether is much more compressed compared to The Overworld, meaning every block travelled in The Nether is equal to eight blocks in The Overworld.

The Soul Sand Valley is an eerie Nether biome filled with soul sand, soul soil, and massive bone fossils. Lit by blue soul fire and shrouded in blue fog, its slow terrain and high spawns of skeletons and ghasts make it highly dangerous to cross. The biome is also home to the Minecraft structure, the Bastion. The Bastion also spawns in 3 types: Treasure, Housing, and Bridges.
