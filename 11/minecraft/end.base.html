Welcome to the End

The End Dimension

A place beyond everything you know. A place where everything floats over the endless void mysteriously. Strange creatures roam this dimension, guarding its secrets and protecting the powerful force that rules this world.

Envronment?

The End is a barren dimension of floating islands hanging above an endless void. Players arrive on a lonely central island surrounded by silence and darkness, with no day or night to guide them. The strange surface of End Stone stretches across the land, while one careless step can send any traveler falling into nothingness.

Remember this guy?

You saw him in the Overworld and in the Nether maybe once or twice. Meet his friends and families in the End. Though safety is not guaranteed among these black guys. Just make sure not to look them directly in the eyes.

The Ender Dragon

The Ender Dragon is the final boss of the game. It flies around the main island and attacks the player from above. The dragon is healed by End Crystals placed on top of tall obsidian pillars, so players must destroy these crystals first. Defeating the dragon unlocks the exit portal and allows access to the outer End islands.

The End?

Even after defeating the Ender Dragon, the journey is far from over. Beyond the central island lie the mysterious End Cities, silent structures hidden across the endless void. Filled with rare treasures, dangerous Shulkers, and ancient designs, they raise one final question: was the End ever truly empty in the first place?
