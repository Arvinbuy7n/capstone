ABOUT AMENTE Founded in Florence, Italy, in 1921, Amente is one of the world’s leading luxury brands. Following the House’s centenary, Amente forges ahead continuing to redefine luxury while celebrating creativity, Italian craftsmanship, and innovation. Amente is part of the global luxury group Kering, which manages renowned Houses in fashion, leather goods, jewelry, and eyewear.
