About Us

Founded in 2019, our MOSAIC newspaper club is a student-led platform dedicated to amplifying student voices and sharing stories that matter. Driven by curiosity and creativity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to amplify student voices and create a platform where ideas, stories and perspectives from our community can be shared. As a student-led newspaper club, we aim to promote honest journalism, creativity, and critical thinking while informing and inspiring our school community.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a vibrant and trusted student newspaper that informs, connects and inspires our school community. We strive to create a space where diverse perspectives are shared and where student journalism continues to grow and make meaningful impact.
