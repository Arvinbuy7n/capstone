Genre: Science Fiction, Drama, Adventure

Setting: Earth and deep space — a near-future where humanity faces extinction

Synopsis:

In a dying world where crops fail and dust storms threaten human survival, former pilot Joseph Cooper is recruited for a daring mission beyond the stars. Alongside a team of scientists, he travels through a mysterious wormhole in search of a new habitable planet for mankind. As time bends and space unfolds in unimaginable ways, Cooper must confront the emotional cost of leaving his family behind—especially his daughter Murph. Interstellar is a powerful story of love, sacrifice, and the limits of human endurance, where the fate of humanity depends on courage and connection across space and time.

Duration: 2 hours 49 minutes (169 minutes)

Cast & Characters:

Matthew McConaughey as Joseph Cooper Anne Hathaway as Amelia Brand Jessica Chastain as Murph (adult) Michael Caine as Professor Brand

Why Watch?

With its breathtaking visuals, emotional depth, and thought-provoking exploration of space and time, Interstellar stands as one of the most ambitious sci-fi films ever made. Directed by Christopher Nolan, the film blends real scientific concepts with powerful storytelling, creating an unforgettable cinematic experience that resonates long after it ends.
