Genre: War, Drama, Historical

Setting: Western Front, World War I — Northern France, 1917

Synopsis:

Amid the brutal trenches of World War I, two young British soldiers, William Schofield and Tom Blake, are given a nearly impossible mission. They must cross enemy territory to deliver a message that could save 1,600 men from walking into a deadly trap. Racing against time, the soldiers face devastating obstacles, constant danger, and the psychological toll of war. As the journey unfolds in what feels like one continuous shot, every step brings them closer to either success—or certain death. 1917 is a powerful and immersive story of courage, sacrifice, and survival, capturing the intensity and humanity of war like never before.

Duration: 1 hour 59 minutes (119 minutes)

Cast & Characters:

MGeorge MacKay as William Schofield Dean-Charles Chapman as Tom Blake Mark Strong as Captain Smith Benedict Cumberbatch as Colonel Mackenzie

Why Watch?

With its groundbreaking “one-shot” cinematography and intense storytelling, 1917 delivers a deeply immersive war experience. Directed by Sam Mendes, the film combines technical brilliance with emotional depth, making it one of the most unforgettable war films ever created.
