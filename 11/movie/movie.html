Genre: Crime, Drama, Historical

Setting: Birmingham, England — post - World War I

Synopsis:

In the aftermath of World War I, Birmingham becomes a city of opportunity—and danger. At the center of it all is Thomas Shelby, a brilliant yet troubled war veteran who rises to power as the leader of the notorious Peaky Blinders gang. Driven by ambition and haunted by his past, Tommy navigates a world of crime, politics, and betrayal. As his empire expands, he faces rival gangs, government forces, and internal struggles that threaten to destroy everything he has built. Peaky Blinders is a gripping story of power, loyalty, and survival in a rapidly changing world—where every decision comes at a cost.

Duration: 55-60 minutes each episode

Cast & Characters:

Cillian Murphy as Thomas Shelby Helen McCrory as Polly Gray Paul Anderson as Arthur Shelby Tom Hardy as Alfie Solomons

Why Watch?

With its intense storytelling, stylish cinematography, and unforgettable performances, Peaky Blinders has become one of the most iconic modern crime dramas. The show blends historical realism with cinematic flair, making every episode feel like a film.
