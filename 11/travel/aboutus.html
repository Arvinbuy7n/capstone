Our Story: In the Heart of the Gobi

Our mission

Since the conception of Three Camel Lodge, it has been our mission to offer explorers the spirit of Mongolia and its nomadic people through authentic experiences while preserving the very earth and culture we proudly showcase. Sustainability is more than a founding principle, it guides every decision of operating the lodge to best take care of the land, the wildlife, Mongolia’s people, culture and traditions.

History

Each guest learns about the nomadic way of life, as well as its traditions and customs, through visits with nomadic families. The gers and furniture is all authentic and created by local Mongolian artisans. Ingredients for the restaurant’s gourmet meals is grown and sourced locally.

Meet Our Team

Most importantly, Three Camel Lodge was built entirely by locals and continues to be staffed entirely by native Mongolians. From our expert hospitality staff to our seasoned field guides, our team brings an intuitive depth of knowledge to welcome you to the desert steppe.

OUR PROMISE

Core Philosophy

🤝

Authentic Nomadic Stewardship

We don't just visit the Gobi; we live within its social fabric. Our journeys are built on reciprocal respect. We support local nomadic cooperatives, ensuring that tourism revenue flows directly into the preservation of traditional pastoralism.

🌲

Leave No Trace, Leave a Legacy

The Gobi is an otherworldly ecosystem of fragile beauty. From our low-impact solar-powered base camps to our strict waste protocols, we ensure the Flaming Cliffs remain pristine. We fund reforestation and water conservation projects.

⭐🖕

Raw Luxury

We redefine luxury. Here, it isn't found in gold-plated faucets. Raw Luxury is the gift of time, space, and absolute silence. It is the warmth of a hand-felted ger after a day of camel trekking under the Milky Way.
