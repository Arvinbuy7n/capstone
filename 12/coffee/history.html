Our History

 

Every great coffee shop has a story. Shifty Shack started with a simple idea: make stressful days better with warm coffee, sweet pastries, and a cozy place where everyone feels welcome.

   
2026
  • Introduced a new seasonal drink menu with special coffee, smoothie, and bubble tea flavors
  • Launched a student-friendly discount program
  • Started offering online ordering and delivery service
  • Opened a redesigned flagship branch with a modern café interior
   
2025
  • Added new desserts, waffles, and cakes to the menu
  • Started using more eco-friendly cups, straws, and packaging
  • Created a loyalty card system for regular customers
  • Hosted small music nights and student events in selected branches
   
2024
  • Expanded the bubble tea menu with fruit tea, milk tea, and tapioca options
  • Introduced new iced coffee flavors for summer
  • Improved the café's interior design with warmer lighting and comfortable seating
  • Started promoting the brand more actively on social media
   
2023
  • Opened new branches in busy shopping and school areas
  • Added more Korean-style drinks and desserts to the menu
  • Started offering special holiday drinks for Christmas and New Year
  • Improved customer service training for employees
   
2022
  • Recovered and expanded services after the difficult pandemic period
  • Started offering more takeaway and delivery-friendly products
  • Introduced new breakfast sets and quick lunch options
  • Added more options for younger customers, including sweet drinks and snacks
   
2021
  • Opened its 40th branch in Mongolia
  • Focused on safe, clean, and comfortable café service
  • Added new cold drinks for summer customers
  • Started special promotions for students and office workers
  • Improved takeaway packaging for coffee and desserts
   
2020
  • Started serving delicious Bubble Tea
  • Opened its 35th branch in Mongolia
   
2019
  • The first branch outside Ulaanbaatar opened in Darkhan city
  • Opened its 30th branch in Mongolia
   
2018
  • Opened Caffe Bene Roastery, the largest coffee shop in Mongolia, at Naadam Center
  • Launched a new Italian gelato ice cream factory
  • Celebrated the 10th anniversary
  • Opened its 25th branch in Ulaanbaatar
   
2017
  • Launched Mongolia's largest coffee roasting factory
  • Opened its 20th branch in Ulaanbaatar
   
2008
  • First branch opened.
  • Integrated production and transportation.
