About CineVerse

CineVerse has been active since 2008. Its founding fathers are Mungunshagai and Aminsetgel. The first movie ever aired on CineVerse was "10 Things I Hate About You". The movie was first released on March 19th, 2008 and since then, CineVerse has released 10,000 more movies, entertaining all of our loyal audience.
