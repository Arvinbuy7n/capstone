F1 NATION: Title predictions and more – 2026 Season Preview

James Hinchcliffe, Jolyon Palmer and Tom Clarkson look ahead to the first race of the 2026 Formula 1 season, the Australian Grand Prix, in the new episode of F1 Nation.

Are Mercedes the favourites? Could Ferrari be title contenders? How will McLaren’s team mate battle develop now Lando Norris is the reigning World Champion?

There are so many questions at the start of a new era of racing. The discussion dives deep into performance, strategy, and whether Red Bull can bounce back strongly this season.

Max Verstappen remains a huge threat, while Ferrari’s strong finishes last year have put them right in the conversation for championship glory.

Meanwhile, McLaren enters the season with confidence after Lando Norris secured his first World Championship, setting up what could be one of the most intense teammate battles in recent history.

With regulation changes shaking things up, unpredictability is at an all-time high. Fans can expect drama, rivalries, and possibly a completely new pecking order.
