Miffy's books are a classic series of children's picture books created by Dutch artist Dick Bruna starting in 1955. Featuring a small, simply drawn rabbit named Miffy (Nijntje), these books are known for their minimalist, bold illustrations and rhyming text, designed for toddlers. Over 30 books have been published, translated into over 50 languages.
