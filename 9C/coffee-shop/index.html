Our Founder

Not in a pretentious way. In the real way. In the hands of people who care about craft. People who love beauty, film, architecture, race cars, surfing, design and dogs. Especially dogs. We roast our own beans and source them the way a museum might curate an exhibition — thoughtfully, obsessively, maybe even a little irrationally. We partner with growers who treat farming like an art form. We build spaces that are part café, part gallery, part daydream. We take coffee seriously. Ourselves? Not so much.

— Erich Joiner, Founder

Sourcing Roasting Recognition

Sourcing

We source our beans from the most renowned coffee-growing regions, working with farmers who share our commitment to quality and sustainability and who believe in equitable and environmentally-responsible practices. By building direct relationships with producers, we enable transparency, fairness and a better cup from the ground up.

Roasting

We roast in small batches, dialing in each coffee to its ideal expression. Light enough to honor the origin. Dark enough to reveal character. Every roast is a conversation between the bean and the heat — and we take that conversation seriously.

The Dark Roast

Our dark roast is bold without being bitter. Rich without being heavy. Developed through years of taste, refinement and an unhealthy obsession with the perfect cup.
