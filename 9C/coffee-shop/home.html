Our Mission

Funiculi Funicula was founded by an artist to bring beautiful coffee experiences to life. Our inspiration? Meeting an interesting new or happy dog. Belly laughs. Imperfect beauty. Genuine smiles. Friendly hangout spots. And yes, a perfectly crafted cup of coffee. We’re here to prove that the little things in life are actually a really big deal. We want to help people get off their phones, away from stress and into conversation, creativity and connection. That’s why we source, roast and brew our coffee with care and craft, because every single cup can be the start of an inspirational idea... one that might even change the world.
