A World Of Diverse Destinations

With over 220 global destinations, InterContinental Hotels & Resorts invites you to explore further. Wherever you stay with us, whether in vibrant cities or tranquil resorts, we offer infinite possibilities for discovery.

Let our newest openings inspire your next journey

InterContinental Crete

Set against a stunning seaside backdrop, this luxurious urban resort on Mirabello Bay offers an iconic escape. InterContinental Crete, open seasonally, serves as your serene island retreat, featuring inspiring infinity pools, exquisite Greek dining options, and refined design.

InterContinental The Red Sea Resort

A barefoot island escape meets Arabian hospitality at InterContinental The Red Sea Resort. Located on Shura Island, the resort is framed by mangroves and sweeping sea views. Experience direct beach access, swimming pools, and signature waterfront vistas.

InterContinental San Antonio Riverwalk

With four distinct restaurants and sweeping city views from its rooftop pool and bar, InterContinental San Antonio Riverwalk offers an unrivalled experience. Stroll along the beautiful network of River Walk pathways, located just steps from our door.

InterContinental Table Bay Cape Town

A refined coastal retreat on the Victoria & Alfred Waterfront, InterContinental Table Bay Cape Town features fine dining, a luxury spa, and an outdoor pool—all set against breathtaking, iconic Table Mountain views.
