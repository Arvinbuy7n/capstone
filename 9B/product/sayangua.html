What is U-Point?

The “UNITED NATIONAL LOYALTY PROGRAM” is a program that allows users to collect and spend reward points from merchants, manufacturers, and importers, supporting your purchases and loyal usage. You can collect and spend 3%-10% of points from all organizations and participate in their reward programs.

By becoming a U-Point customer

  • Use only one card or application instead of multiple cards from multiple organizations.
  • The points collected from the purchase will be deducted from the amount of the next purchase to receive a discount.
  • You can participate in interesting incentive programs and enjoy additional discounts.

Birthday bonus:

Birthday bonus: Upoint Collective team members can enjoy a 25% discount at our UID, Chandman Center (behind the Central Library), and Emart (Khan-Uul) branches by showing their discount code in the “My Coupon” section.

Warning:

Please note that this discount is only valid in the above 3 branches and that pre-ordering and delivery are not possible while enjoying the discount.
