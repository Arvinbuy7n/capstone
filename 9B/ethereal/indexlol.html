Game Overview

Resident Evil Requiem is a 2026 survival horror game focused on a dual narrative between FBI analyst Grace Ashcroft and agent Leon S. Kennedy. Gameplay switches between Grace’s slower stealth-heavy survival sections and Leon’s more aggressive combat encounters. The story centers around a disturbing investigation tied to an abandoned hotel filled with biological horrors and hidden secrets.
