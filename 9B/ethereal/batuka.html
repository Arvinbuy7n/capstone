Rennsport

Drive like you have never before

Description

RENNSPORT is a high-performance racing simulation game developed by Competition Company and Teyon. Designed as a photo-realistic "playground" for car enthusiasts, it focuses heavily on professional-grade driving dynamics and esports competition.

Release & Platforms

Officially released for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Engine & Visuals

Utilizes Unreal Engine 5 for ultra-high-fidelity visuals alongside laser-scanned accurate tracks.

Esports & Realism

Features competitive multiplayer networks (ESL R1) designed explicitly alongside input from pro drivers.
