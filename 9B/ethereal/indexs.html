The Devil is Dead

Set in 1925, The Lightkeeper is a psychological horror game following Arthur Lucian, a veteran seeking peace as a remote lighthouse keeper.

The gameplay blends routine maritime maintenance with a slow-burn descent into madness as Arthur’s traumatic past and the island’s dark history collide. Players must manage the lighthouse while uncovering cryptic clues, eventually confronting "monsters" born from Arthur’s own isolation and guilt.
