Phu Quoc

your island escape awaits

Escape to InterContinental Phu Quoc Long Beach Resort, where turquoise seas meet lush jungle hills. Enjoy elegant suites and villas, exceptional dining, award-winning spa experiences, and refined Vietnamese-inspired luxury on the stunning shores of Phu Quoc Island.

A must-visit attraction in Phu Quoc, this fairytale-like amusement park offers unlimited access to all rides with your ticket, plus reasonably priced food. The stunning scenery is impressive throughout, but the aquarium is easily the highlight of the experience.

The main entrance opens to a charming Venice-inspired street lined with shops and canals, creating a lively atmosphere. Visitors can also purchase a VIP cruise experience for 400,000 VND. Don’t miss the local fruits — they’re exceptionally fresh, sweet, and juicy. The Teddy Bear Museum is another highlight, featuring unique teddy bear designs from different eras and cultures.
