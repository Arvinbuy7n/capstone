What Is Baymax Bonus Card?

10%

Baymax Bonus Card is a card issued by Baymax Cinema with the aim of rewarding and caring for its loyal customers. Anyone who becomes a user of this card will be able to collect 10% of the purchase price in their bonus card when making a purchase in certain designated areas of Baymax Cinema. They will also be entitled to see one free movie on their birthday.

What Is Baymax Bonus Card?

15%

Baymax Bonus Card is a card issued by Baymax Cinema with the aim of rewarding and caring for its loyal customers. Anyone who becomes a user of this card will be able to collect 15% of the purchase price in their bonus card when making a purchase in certain designated areas of Baymax Cinema. They will also be entitled to see one free movie on their birthday.

Bonus card validity period

The current cinema membership card is valid for 3 years from the date of opening. Expired users will not be credited with bonus tugriks for any purchases. Also, expired users will not be credited with bonus tugriks.

Bonus card price

A new bonus card member will be charged 2500 tugriks, and the user can use this card for 3 years.

Conditions under which bonus tugriks cannot be collected

If the user uses any gift card or gift ticket, bonus tugriks will not be considered. Bonus tugriks cannot be collected on discount gift cards and gift tickets issued by the cinema.
